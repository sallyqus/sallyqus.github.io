About

I’m a DPhil researcher in Genomic Medicine and Statistics at the University of Oxford, supported by a Wellcome Trust Doctoral Award. My work explores how machine learning can help us understand complex biological systems—from single-cell data to genetic perturbations.

Alongside research, I lead AI development at OxValue AI, an Oxford spin-out. I bring together technical, product, and operational work to turn AI capabilities into usable products. Previously, I was an early team member at Eigent AI.
